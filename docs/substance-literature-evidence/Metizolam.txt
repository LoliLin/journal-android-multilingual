=== Detection of the designer benzodiazepine metizolam in urine and preliminary data on its metabolism.

    ? 2017 | PMID 27671107 | DOI 10.1002/dta.2099
    - About 0.3% of the initial dose was excreted in urines as total unchanged metizolam during the first 24 h.
    - Three mono-hydroxylated metabolites were produced including a hydroxylation compound at the 2-ethyl moiety of metizolam (M1) as quantitatively main metabolite, and a N-hydroxymetiazolam (M2).
