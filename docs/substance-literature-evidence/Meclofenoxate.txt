For instance, deanol (DMAE) and meclofenoxate serve as choline precursors, supporting acetylcholine synthesis and potentially improving learning and memory, with suggested daily intakes ranging from 500 to 2000 mg. Nicergoline, an ergot derivative, is thought to improve cognitive function, mood, and alertness and is typically administered at 30–60 mg/day. Piracetam, a well-known nootropic, modulates calcium and potassium ion channel activity, enhancing neuronal excitability; it is used acutely at 4–8 g/day, with maintenance doses between 2 and 4 g/day. Pyritinol, a derivative of vitamin B6, may enhance choline acetyltransferase activity and is administered at a minimum of 300 mg/day, though higher doses ( 600 mg/day) are often used clinically.
