LC–neg–ESI–MS in selective ion monitoring (SIM) mode for m/z 127 (iodide), combined with in-source fragmentation, was used to verify iodinated TPs by comparing retention times in LC–pos–ESI–MS and LC–neg–ESI–MS chromatograms (Putschew and Jekel 2003 ; Hütteroth et al. 2007 ). All samples were filtered (0.45 µm) immediately after sampling for analysis, with AAS samples further acidified. Compared to the results of the ZWTs, these values are considerably higher (Table 1 ), indicating a significantly slower transformation of the IOP derivatives in the aquifer material–water tests under nitrate-reducing conditions, as well as a pronounced adaptation period. This extended lag phase may also be related to the presence of biodegradable DOC in the pore water (Table B.1.1), which may have been preferentially metabolized by the microbial community prior to IOP.
