=== Worsening of the Toxic Effects of (±)<i>Cis</i>-4,4'-DMAR Following Its Co-Administration with (±)<i>Trans</i>-4,4'-DMAR: Neuro-Behavioural, Physiological, Immunohistochemical and Metabolic Studies in Mice.
    Tirri M, Frisoni P, Bilel S, Arfè R, Trapella C, Fantinati A, Corli G, Marchetti B, De-Giorgio F, Camuto C, Mazzarino M, Gaudio RM, Serpelloni G, Schifano F, Botrè F, Marti M.
