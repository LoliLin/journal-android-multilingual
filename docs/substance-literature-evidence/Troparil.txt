=== Characterization and Metabolism of Drug Products Containing the Cocaine-Like New Psychoactive Substances Indatraline and Troparil.
    Manier SK, Mumber P, Zapp J, Eckstein N, Meyer MR.
    ? 2024 | PMID 38921476 | DOI 10.3390/metabo14060342
    - Indatraline formed two phase I and four phase II metabolites, with aromatic hydroxylation and glucuronidation being the main metabolic steps.
    - Troparil formed four phase I and three phase II metabolites, with demethylation being the main metabolic step.
    - Hydroxylation of the tropane ring, the phenyl ring, and combinations of these steps, as well as glucuronidation, were found.
