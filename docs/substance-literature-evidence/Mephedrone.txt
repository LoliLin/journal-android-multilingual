=== Cross-species metabolic characterization of eutylone and mechanism-based inhibition of CYP2D6 and CYP2B6 with drug interaction implications.
    Lee MS, Song IS, Jang Y, Cho YY, Lee JY, Kang HC, Kim JH, Lee HS.
    ? 2026 | PMID 41991640 | PMCID PMC13243497 | DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-48814-7
    https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/13243497
    - Eutylone exhibited moderate plasma protein binding ( f u ≈ 0.4–0.5), greater binding than methylone or mephedrone in rats ( f u ≈ 0.70–0.78) but less than benzedrone or chlorinated analogues ( f u ≈ 0.20–0.33 in humans) 30 – 32 . This pattern is consistent with its intermediate lipophilicity and methylenedioxyphenyl scaffold, which can increase the interactions with albumin and alpha-1-acid glycoprotein relative to simple para-substituted cathinones. Using f u -corrected IVIVE, hepatic extraction was predicted to be moderate in humans, mice, and dogs, but high in rats, indicating the efficient first-pass elimination of eutylone in rats compared to humans. Such differences are particularly relevant for regulatory decision-making, because rats are often used as preclinical models to infer human risk for emerging psychoactive substances. Characterization of the full metabolite profile across species is therefore essential to understanding these differences, and was pursued using an untargeted FBMN-guided approach.
